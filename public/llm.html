JAY-23 — Machine-Readable Profile

JAY-23 is a founder-led prelaunch marketing agency that runs the 90-day MVA (Minimum Viable Audience) Framework, helping crowdfunding creators reach 1,000 true fans before launch.

About

JAY23 LLC operates from Buffalo, Wyoming (USA) and serves clients worldwide in English and Polish. Founded by Marek Cieśla. Track record: 46 campaigns, $1.2M+ raised on Kickstarter and Indiegogo.

Services

  • MVA Framework (90 days) — Discover, Build, Launch phases. $1,500/month (EN) or 6,000 PLN/month (PL).
  • Meta Ads — paid acquisition tuned for crowdfunding prelaunch.
  • Email funnels — automated nurture sequences for backers.
  • MVA Quiz — interactive lead magnet that segments audience by readiness.
  • Community activation — private group warming up to launch day.

How the 90-day program works

  1. Discover (days 1–30) — niche research, audience definition, message-market fit.
  2. Build (days 31–60) — landing page, quiz, email funnel, ad creatives.
  3. Launch (days 61–90) — paid traffic, community activation, conversion to backers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is JAY-23?

What is the MVA Framework?

MVA stands for Minimum Viable Audience. It is a 90-day, three-phase program (Discover, Build, Launch) that combines Meta Ads, email funnels, an interactive quiz, and a private community to convert cold traffic into 1,000 launch-day backers.

The MVA program is $1,500/month for English-speaking founders and 6,000 PLN/month for Polish founders. Total program length is 3 months. Full pricing at jay23.com/en/packages.

What results has JAY-23 delivered?

Who is JAY-23 for?

JAY-23 works with hardware, lifestyle, and consumer-product founders preparing a Kickstarter, Indiegogo, or direct-to-consumer pre-order campaign who want a predictable system to build their launch audience.
