# JAY-23 Process - 90 Days to a Paying Audience

> Three-phase program that builds your Minimum Viable Audience before launch. Each phase builds on the last.

## Overview

90 days. A paying audience, before you launch. Three focused months, each with specific deliverables owned by either the agency or the client.

---

## Phase 01 / Build - Month 1 (Weeks 1-4)

**Persona mapping and quiz strategy** *(Agency)*
Define 1-3 buyer personas. Build a dedicated quiz path for each, with a tailored PDF report as the incentive and a unique funnel track.

**Landing page on prelaunch.live** *(Agency)*
Quiz - email capture - Thank You page with WhatsApp VIP QR. Full Meta Pixel + GA4 setup. Every visitor is cookied for remarketing.

**WhatsApp VIP Group + account access** *(Client)*
Client creates the VIP group and grants access to Meta, TikTok/Google, Stripe, MailerLite.

**7-mail sequence + Stripe $1 flow** *(Agency)*
Full MailerLite automation. Mail 1 delivers the report and the Early Bird reservation link. Webhook moves paying leads into a dedicated segment.

---

## Phase 02 / Drive - Month 2 (Weeks 5-8)

**Ads live - Andromeda framework** *(Agency)*
Meta + TikTok on Standard package. Google Search + PMax on Starter. Videos use the comment-CTA mechanic through ManyChat into the quiz. A/B hook testing from day one.

**Community activation** *(Agency + Client)*
Agency manages ads. Client runs the VIP group with 3+ posts per week, polls, sneak peeks, and Q&A.

**Weekly optimization and creative rotation** *(Agency)*
New creatives every two weeks. Quiz drop-off analysis. Google Display remarketing. Weekly CPL and Stripe conversion rate reports.

---

## Phase 03 / Launch - Month 3 (Weeks 9-12)

**Lookalike campaigns from paying segment** *(Agency)*
Paying $1 subscribers become a Meta Custom Audience, then a 1-3% Lookalike. Google Customer Match.

**Full launch campaign across all channels** *(Agency)*
Launch email to full list plus exclusive offer to the paying segment. Ads target Custom Audiences and Lookalikes.

**Final report and handover** *(Agency + Client)*
MVA report covering leads, paying segment, ROAS, funnel conversion rate per stage. All access and documentation transferred.

---

## Guaranteed results

- 300+ qualified leads by end of month 3
- 30+ paying Early Bird subscribers before launch
- Max cost per lead: 25 PLN
- 3 channels: ads, email, WhatsApp community

If by end of month 1 you don't have at least 50 subscribers and first Early Bird payments, the program is extended for free until you do.
