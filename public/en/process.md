# 90-Day MVA Process — JAY-23

> 90 days. A paying audience, before you launch. Three focused months. Each phase builds on the last.

## Phase 01 / Build — Month 1 (Weeks 1–4)

- **Persona mapping + quiz strategy** (Agency): Define 1–3 buyer personas, dedicated quiz path, tailored PDF report as incentive, unique funnel track.
- **Landing page on prelaunch.live** (Agency): Quiz → email capture → Thank You page with WhatsApp VIP QR. Full Meta Pixel + GA4 setup. Visitor cookied for remarketing.
- **WhatsApp VIP Group + account access** (Client): Client creates the VIP group, grants access to Meta, TikTok/Google, Stripe, MailerLite.
- **7-mail sequence + Stripe $1 flow** (Agency): Full MailerLite automation. Mail 1 delivers report + Early Bird reservation link. Webhook moves paying leads into dedicated segment.

## Phase 02 / Drive — Month 2 (Weeks 5–8)

- **Ads live — Andromeda framework** (Agency): Meta + TikTok (Standard) or Google Search + PMax (Starter). Videos use comment-CTA mechanic → ManyChat → quiz. A/B hook testing from day 1.
- **Community activation** (Agency + Client): Agency manages ads. Client runs VIP group — 3+ posts/week, polls, sneak peeks, Q&A.
- **Weekly optimization + creative rotation** (Agency): New creatives every 2 weeks. Quiz drop-off analysis. Google Display remarketing. Weekly CPL and Stripe CR reports.

## Phase 03 / Launch — Month 3 (Weeks 9–12)

- **Lookalike campaigns from paying segment** (Agency): Paying $1 subscribers → Meta Custom Audience → 1–3% Lookalike. Google Customer Match.
- **Full launch campaign — all channels** (Agency): Launch email to full list + exclusive offer to paying segment. Ads target Custom Audiences + Lookalike.
- **Final report + handover** (Agency + Client): MVA report: leads, paying segment, ROAS, funnel CR per stage. All access and documentation transferred.

## Guaranteed Results

- 300+ qualified leads by end of month 3
- 30+ Paying Early Bird subscribers before launch
- Max cost per lead: 25 PLN
- 3 channels: ads, email, WhatsApp community
