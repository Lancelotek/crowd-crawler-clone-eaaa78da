# About JAY-23

> Growth marketing studio specializing in prelaunch strategies and Minimum Viable Audience building. 50+ startups served, $1M+ raised in crowdfunding.

## Who we are

JAY-23 is a growth marketing studio focused on one thing: getting founders to launch with a paying audience instead of silence.

We built the MVA (Minimum Viable Audience) Framework because we kept watching great products die on launch day. Not from bad products, from empty lists.
