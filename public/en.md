## The Cold Launch Trap

97% of product launches fail. Not because the product was bad — because Meta had nobody to show it to. The Cold Launch Trap kills 97% of product launches before they start — not on launch day, but on the day the founder decided to build first and find customers later.

The algorithm needs signal. Signal comes from an engaged audience. An engaged audience takes 90 days to build.

## The MVA Framework

The MVA (Minimum Viable Audience) Framework reverses the order. You build the audience first. Meta gets signal from day one. You launch to people who already paid $1 to be on your list.

**Old way:** Build product → Find audience (after launch) → Run ads to cold traffic → High CPC, low CVR → $180 cost per customer

**MVA way:** Research demand first → Build $1 Early Bird waitlist → Warm audience before ads → Meta gets signal from engaged subscribers → Run ads to Lookalikes → $12-25 cost per customer
